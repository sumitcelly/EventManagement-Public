The Golden Rule for Connect:
Stripe always takes its processing fee out of the total amount charged to the customer. 

It then sends your application_fee_amount to you, and the "leftovers" go to the organizer.

The Financial Math:
When organizer absorbs stripe fees

This is actually the simplest way to configure Stripe Connect.
The Financial Workflow
Ticket Price: $20.00
Platform Fee (Line Item): $1.00
Customer Pays: $21.00
ApplicationFeeAmount: $1.00 (This goes to you).
Stripe Fee: ~$0.91 (Calculated on the $21.00).
Organizer Receives: $19.09 ($21.00 - $1.00 - $0.91)


When organizer passes Stripe fees to customer:
Ticket: $20.00
Service Fee: $1.95 (Your $1.00 profit + $0.95 to cover Stripe)
Customer Pays: $21.95
Organizer Gets: ~$20.01 (They are happy)
